Particle Physics & Cosmology

I work at the intersection of particle physics and cosmology, where the smallest constituents of matter shape the largest structures in the universe. My research follows the threads connecting high-energy processes in the early universe to the patterns we observe today — in the cosmic microwave background, the distribution of galaxies, and the terrestrial observatories.

I'm drawn to the open questions that sit at this boundary: what dark matter is made of, how the universe's structure first took shape, and what particle physics can tell us about the conditions of the very early cosmos.
