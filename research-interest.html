Research interest

Where particles meet the cosmos.

My work sits across particle physics and cosmology, using the physics of fundamental interactions to understand how the universe evolved and what it's made of. Below are the threads I currently follow most closely.

Early-universe particle cosmology

How high-energy particle processes in the first moments after the Big Bang shaped the universe we observe today — from primordial abundances to the seeds of structure.

  • Thermal history of the early universe
  • Baryogenesis and leptogenesis
  • Signatures in the cosmic microwave background

Dark matter and beyond the Standard Model

Probing what dark matter could be made of, and what new particles or symmetries beyond the Standard Model might explain it.

  • Dark matter candidates and detection strategies
  • Extensions of the Standard Model
  • Hidden-sector interactions
